# who_keeps_meaning_study — 不工作之后，谁撑得住？

Pre-registered analysis of GSS 1972–2024 + 2006–2014 panels: which resources (friends, religious attendance,
marriage, income) buffer the drop in "finding life exciting" when prime-age adults are not working, and does it
differ by life stage. Stage 1 of Paper 2; stage 2 feeds the estimates into the Paper 1 ABM in place of assumed
coefficients.

- `PREREGISTRATION.md` — frozen before outcomes were looked at (commit time = timestamp).
- `DEVIATIONS.md` — any change after freezing.
- `scripts/` — analysis code; `scripts/DATA_SOURCES.md` — where the data comes from.
- `RESULTS.md` — written after the run.
