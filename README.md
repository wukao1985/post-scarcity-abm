# Meaning in the Post-AI Era

A lifelong inquiry into **human meaning when AI automates much of human work** — and what,
on the level of policy, culture, and psychology, we can prepare. Plus the empirical studies
and archived work that feed it.

Start with [`meaning_project/MANIFESTO.md`](meaning_project/MANIFESTO.md).

| Path | What |

## Principle

Credibility is the only capital an independent scholar compounds. So: never dress a guess as a
fact, verify sources from primary text, pre-register empirical work, and let the data win —
even when it kills the hypothesis. See [`LESSONS.md`](LESSONS.md).
