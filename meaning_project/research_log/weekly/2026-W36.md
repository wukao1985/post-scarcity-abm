## The week in one paragraph

**The macro-null met its next pre-committed test and passed convincingly — while the high-exposure composition
story it sits beside not only held but printed one of its sharper months — and, as a bonus, W32's scary headline
reversed on revision.** The August jobs report (BLS, Sep 4) came in at **+162,000 payrolls** with unemployment
**unchanged at 4.1%** — a *strong* beat (consensus was ~53K; the biggest gain since March), i.e. the aggregate
US labour market looks, if anything, *healthier* than expected, not weaker. Three things inside the print matter
for this project. **(1)** No AI-attributable aggregate harm: the awaited test resolved to a healthy top-line with,
as always, **no BLS AI attribution**. **(2)** The composition story sharpened rather than faded: **information
shed −23,000 in a single month** — with the loss located precisely in the sectors the project tracks (computing
infrastructure / data processing / web hosting **−8K**, publishing **−7K**, broadcasting & content **−5K**) —
while **financial activities showed little change** (no acceleration, consistent with the standing pattern). So
the month is one of the cleanest single-print instances yet of the project's recurring shape: **aggregate calm
(indeed strength) sitting directly on top of continued, concentrated, high-exposure-sector decline.** **(3)** The
methodology gift: **July's −23,000 was revised up to +21,000** (and June up to +31K), a combined +55K two-month
revision. W32's "first outright negative payroll month" — the print six dailies had flagged as a possible
inflection and which `concepts.md` recorded the decoupling as having "held through" — has now **literally
reversed on revision**: the scary headline was a revision artifact, not a signal. That is the sharpest possible
vindication of the "don't over-read a single negative print" discipline the project keeps preaching — the print
didn't merely resolve to education/retail, it *flipped positive*. Against that one real update, the rest of the
sweep was the discipline of *not* logging: AI-companion searches returned only the already-anchored Zhang et al.
/ Folk & Dunn / Aalto cluster (no new study); UBI/wellbeing returned old UK microsimulation modelling and
already-anchored pilots (no new primary result); NBER labour hits were all already-anchored (Yotzov w34836,
Baslandze/executives w34984, Karger forecasting w35046) or theoretical; **no new Anthropic Economic Index** since
June's "Cadences" (confirmed); and no new primary post-labour essay of note.
