## The week in one paragraph

**A genuinely quiet, consolidating week — and the sharpest possible contrast with W27.** Where W27
was an attribution-cluster week (five different instruments converging on one shape), W28 produced
**no new empirical evidence at all** on any core thread. The macro-null didn't move (no jobs data —
the next Employment Situation release is 7 Aug); the entry-level puzzle didn't move; UBI/wellbeing
produced only pilot *launch* announcements (Howard County phase two, Cook County, Cambridge,
Boulder), no new outcomes; the relatedness cluster was silent. The augmentation/automation-as-
psychology thread gained one thin, single-country survey (a Chinese SEM study naming "workplace AI
replacement anxiety" as a mediator inside the already-anchored ILO-WP170 autonomy-erosion pathway) —
texture, not a revision. The honest read is that the dense evidence flow of June and early July has
**paused**, and a disciplined log's job in such a week is to *say so* rather than manufacture
significance from recirculated headlines. The one thing worth the week's attention is meta: the log
**caught its own miss.** The UN's first global, IPCC-style independent scientific assessment of AI
(40 scientists, every region; preliminary, ahead of a full 2027 report) slipped past a dozen
consecutive daily scans. Its labour framing sits squarely on this project's watchlist variables —
it makes the "new jobs" mechanism **explicitly conditional** ("*with* complementary investments in
skills, workflows, infrastructure and labour-market institutions, technology can create new jobs
that do not exist right now"; *without* them, AI "risks widening inequality, displacing workers and
shifting wealth from labour to capital"), and frames good outcomes around "**worker autonomy** and a
reliable path to social **dignity**." That is the project's own economic–meaning decoupling and its
new-jobs-precondition concept, stated by a UN-convened scientific panel. It deserves a dedicated
primary-text read, not a rushed same-week entry — so the substantive move this week is to **flag it
properly and schedule the read**, and to record honestly that nothing else happened.

## Notable items

- **UN Independent International Scientific Panel on AI — Preliminary Report** (United Nations;
  released **1 Jul 2026**; 40 scientists/experts from every region; **preliminary**, ahead of a full
  2027 report). 🟡 (institutional evidence-based synthesis; **full primary report not yet read** —
  labour specifics below are from the UN press release, UN News, and secondary coverage; flagged for
  a dedicated primary-text session). *What it shows (labour-relevant):* the first global, IPCC-style
  independent scientific assessment of AI opportunities, risks and impacts. On employment it takes an
  explicitly **conditional** line — AI "*will likely create new jobs*" **only with** "complementary
  investments in skills … workflows, infrastructure and labour-market institutions"; **without** them
  it "risks widening inequality, displacing workers and shifting wealth from **labour to capital**
  rather than creating sustainable good jobs — those with fair compensation, **worker autonomy** and a
  reliable path to social **dignity**." It invokes the **"60% of 2018 jobs are new vs 1945"**
  new-jobs precedent, and flags that agentic AI "will soon complete tasks that currently take human
  programmers days or weeks," raising "urgent questions for labour markets." *Significance for the
  meaning niche:* a UN scientific panel independently states two of the project's load-bearing moves —
  (1) the **new-jobs mechanism is a precondition, not a law** (it holds only if the complementary
  investment is made), and (2) the outcome that matters is not just headcount but **autonomy and
  dignity** (the economic–meaning decoupling, in institutional language). It does not add a 🟢 datum,
  but it is the most citable *institutional* backing yet for the framing this project is built on.
  *Limits:* preliminary; a synthesis/assessment, not primary research; the labour section is a small
  part of a governance-and-safety-weighted document; full text unread. *Action:* logged as a **pending
  primary read** (evidence_base flag), to be written up properly in a dedicated session.
  https://www.un.org/independent-international-scientific-panel-ai/en/preliminary-report ·
  https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167853

- **Algorithmic management → frustrated autonomy → "AI replacement anxiety" → worse health (China
  SEM survey)** — *Frontiers in Digital Health* 8:1877221 (accepted 7 Jul 2026, published this week).
  🟡 (cross-sectional survey, N=338 Chinese employees; structural-equation modelling with bootstrap;
