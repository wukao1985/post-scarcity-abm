## Watchlist movement

- **New to log (outside window, March 10, 2026):** *"Evaluating a 'UBI Plus' Intervention:
  A Needs-based Analysis of WorkFREE"* — Social Indicators Research 182(1), DOI:
  10.1007/s11205-026-03806-y; PMC12975856.
  🟡 (peer-reviewed, Max-Neef needs framework, mixed-methods; **Indian slum context** —
  1,400 residents, INR 500–1,000/month cash, so dollar amounts and context are not directly
  comparable to Western UBI pilots; primary text not retrieved directly).
  *What it shows:* WorkFREE combined unconditional cash transfers with monthly community
  "Plus meetings" (needs-focused collective spaces) for 18 months. Compared to the companion
  HudsonUP study (cash only), the "UBI Plus" format showed stronger gains across *both*
  material and psychological/relational needs — described as "synergic power of UBI Plus."
  The finding: cash alone raised material-need satisfaction but left relational and
  psychological needs only partially addressed; structured community organising alongside
  cash was significantly more effective for those dimensions.
  *Relevance for the meaning project:* Directly relevant to the UBI psychological-outcomes
  thread and to the project's core question — it provides modest evidence that the missing
  ingredient is not just income but *structured social participation*. This echoes Jahoda
  (latent functions = time structure, collective purpose, social contact) and the German RCT's
  "purpose-in-life" gain: what may supply the purpose dividend is not the cash per se but
  what the cash enables or is bundled with. **Limits to note honestly:** Indian slum pilot is
  far from the post-labour OECD scenario; the "Plus" intervention is hard to define scalably;
  Max-Neef framework is normative, not SDT. Not an evidence-base candidate yet (Indian context
  too disanalogous; primary text unverified); worth monitoring if a Western replication emerges.
  → idea: "the bundle hypothesis" — if purpose gains require *bundled* social structures (not
  just cash), that reframes the post-labour policy question: what are the institutions that do
  for post-work what workplaces used to do for the bundle?
