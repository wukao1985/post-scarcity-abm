## Items

- **Chi, Rietsche, Göldi, Ungar & Guntuku, "Optimized but Unowned: How AI-Authored Goals Undermine
  the Motivation They Are Meant to Drive"** — arXiv:2605.12344 (submitted 12 May 2026, rev. 13 May
  2026; Univ. of Pennsylvania / Bern Univ. of Applied Sciences). 🟢 **preregistered experiment,
  with caveats** (N=470; two-week behavioural follow-up; preprint — not yet peer-reviewed;
  single-session lab-style goal-setting task, not workplace field data). *Shows:* participants
  given **LLM-authored** personal goals (vs self-authored) ended up with objectively *better-formed*
  goals by SMART criteria (d=2.26) but reported **lower psychological ownership** (d=1.38),
  commitment (d=1.19) and perceived importance (d=1.13) — and at 2-week follow-up, only **46.6%**
  had acted on ≥2 goals vs **72.8%** of the self-authored group. Mediation analysis: **psychological
  ownership**, not objective goal quality, explains the gap — authorship changes whether the person
  experiences the plan as *theirs*. Notably, people **low in trait self-efficacy** (the ones most
  likely to reach for AI help) showed the **steepest** ownership erosion — a possible AI-inequality
  channel (those least equipped to compensate lose the most). *Limits:* preprint, single lab task
  (not field/workplace), short 2-week horizon, US/university-adjacent sample likely, no
  income/employment context. *Why it matters:* this is a second, independent, pre-registered
  **causal** test of the same mechanism Lee et al. (*Sci Rep* 2026, already anchored) found for
  *task execution* — AI authorship of an identity-relevant artifact corrodes ownership and downstream
  motivation regardless of the artifact's objective quality — but here applied to **goal-setting**
  itself, one level upstream of task execution. Strengthens the "how you use AI, not whether"
   psychological thread with a second, distinct paradigm. → **candidate for evidence_base** (pairs
  directly with Lee et al. under "How AI is used governs its meaning effect").
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12344
