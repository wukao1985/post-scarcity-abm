## Items

- **Algorithmic management → frustrated autonomy → AI-replacement anxiety → worse health (chain
  mediation, China survey)** — Frontiers in Digital Health 8:1877221 (accepted 7 Jul 2026,
  published this week). 🟡. Cross-sectional survey of 338 Chinese employees across industries;
  structural equation modelling with bootstrap resampling. *Shows:* perceived algorithmic
  management predicts worse physical/mental health via two mediators — frustrated work autonomy
  and a newly-named "workplace AI replacement anxiety" — both independently and in a sequential
  chain (algorithmic management → frustrated autonomy → replacement anxiety → health). *Limits:*
  single-country (China), self-report, cross-sectional (no causal inference), standard Frontiers
  survey-SEM tier — not a pillar. *Why it matters:* names and measures a specific psychological
  construct ("AI replacement anxiety") sitting *inside* the already-anchored ILO WP170
  autonomy-erosion pathway, giving it a testable mediator and a health outcome rather than just a
  qualitative synthesis. Texture, not a revision — pairs with ILO WP170 and Lee et al. on the
  "AI deployed *on* workers erodes autonomy" thread. Not flagged as evidence_base material (too
  thin alone); noted for possible future synthesis if a non-China replication appears.
