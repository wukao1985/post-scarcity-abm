# Idea Pool — incubating article ideas

A holding pen for Medium article ideas. Promote the ripe ones to `articles/`. Kill the weak
ones honestly. Tag each with the dominant claim-type it would make: 🟢 empirical / 🟡 analogy /
🔴 design. Good essays mix them but should *lead* with a clear spine.

Status: 💡 raw · 🌱 developing · ✍️ drafting · ✅ published

---

## Tier 1 — strongest candidates

- 💡 🟡 **"How to honestly talk about the future of AI" — a three-layer framework.**
  The signature methodology piece. Introduces 🟢/🟡/🔴. Uses the SO null result as a worked
  example of letting data kill your hypothesis. Establishes the author's brand: calibrated
  honesty. *Strong candidate for FIRST article.*

- 💡 🟢 **"Money doesn't buy meaning — what UBI experiments actually measured."**
  Synthesizes the UBI/lottery/retirement evidence (evidence_base.md). The core recurring
  finding, told carefully. A 🟢-anchored piece that also sets up the whole project's thesis.

- 💡 🟡 **"Is AI like the Industrial Revolution? Three places the analogy breaks."**
  What is automated (muscle→routine cognition→cognition/judgment itself); speed vs adjustment
  timescale; whether the "new jobs appear" mechanism still works if AI does the new jobs too.
  Turns a vague debate into a watchlist of trackable variables.

## Tier 2 — develop later

- 💡 🟢 **"The latent functions of work — and which ones AI threatens."** Jahoda, applied
  function-by-function to a post-labour world. Which functions are substitutable (income via
  UBI) vs sticky (status, purpose, structure)?
- 💡 🔴 **"Robust policies for a post-labour society: what holds up across many futures."**
  Robust-decision-making framing. Not prediction — which arrangements are good bets regardless.
- 💡 🟡 **"The retreat that ran out of high ground."** The historical pattern of humans retreating
  to higher cognitive ground after each automation wave — and why this time may have no next
  rung.
- 💡 🟢 **"What FIRE and early retirees can teach us about post-work meaning."** Could become a
  real small empirical pillar (analyze public community data / surveys).
- 💡 🔴 **"Designing for mattering: meaning infrastructure as public policy."** If contribution-
  to-others is the part virtual/UBI can't replace, what would institutions that manufacture
  "mattering" look like?

## Parking lot (raw sparks)
