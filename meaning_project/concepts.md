# Concepts — the project's own coinages & frameworks

As the writing develops, the goal is a few signature concepts others can cite — the move from
"person who writes essays" to "person who proposed a framework." Seed them here; refine as
they recur across articles.

Status: 🌰 seed · 🌿 used in ≥1 article · 🌳 signature (recurs, others cite)

## 🌰 The three-layer honesty framework (🟢/🟡/🔴)
Empirical / analogical / normative-design claims, never blurred. The project's methodological
spine. → likely the first article.

## 🌰 The economic–meaning decoupling
Even in the most economically optimistic post-labour future (jobs appear, UBI is ample), the
*meaning* question stays fully open. Separating these two debates is itself a contribution,
because ~99% of discourse conflates them.

## 🌰 "Money buys relief, not meaning"
The recurring 🟢 finding from cash-transfer/lottery/retirement evidence: money reliably removes
material stress but is a weak lever on purpose/meaning.

## 🌰 The retreat that may run out of high ground
Each automation wave pushed humans to higher cognitive ground; AI targets that ground itself.
The open question of whether a "next rung" exists. (Analogy with an explicit breakpoint.)

## 🌰 The "new-jobs mechanism" precondition
The historical reassurance ("new jobs always appear") secretly assumes humans are better than
machines at the new jobs. Name and interrogate that precondition.

## 🌰 Meaning infrastructure / manufacturing "mattering"
If contribution-to-others is what UBI and virtual worlds can't supply, what would institutions
that deliberately produce "mattering" look like? (Design / 🔴.)
