# Manifesto — The Meaning Project

*A lifelong inquiry into human meaning in the post-AI / post-labour era.*

Last updated: 2026-05-31. This is a living document.

---

## The question

As AI automates a growing share of cognitive work, what happens to **human meaning** —
not just income, but identity, competence, belonging, status, and purpose — and what, if
anything, can we prepare on the level of **policy, culture, and psychology**?

This is deliberately *not* primarily the economics question (will there be jobs, can we afford
UBI). Economists and policy shops own that. The neglected question — and this project's home
turf — is: **even in the most economically optimistic future, can a society where most people
no longer work sustain psychological health and social cohesion? And if the latent functions
of work disappear, where does meaning come from instead?**

## Why this niche

"AI and jobs" is one of the most crowded topics of the decade. "AI and *meaning*" is nearly
empty by comparison — yet it (a) has a real empirical base to stand on, (b) spans the
psychological / cultural / policy layers the author actually cares about, and (c) suits an
independent scholar (it needs synthesis, framing, and persistent questioning, not a big lab).

**Scope: "human meaning in the post-labour era."** Narrow enough to own; deep enough for a
lifetime.

## The method: three kinds of claim, never blurred

The core discipline of this project — and its differentiator in a field drowning in false
certainty — is to always mark which kind of claim is being made:

- 🟢 **Empirical (falsifiable).** What we already know, with data. Held to the standard of
  real science: pre-registration, placebo/controls, the data gets to win. *(e.g. the
  psychological results of real UBI experiments; deaths of despair; retirement studies.)*
- 🟡 **Analogical (structured inference).** What we can argue from history and mechanism — with
  the breakpoints of the analogy stated explicitly. The value is not "so the future will be
  X" but "so watch *this* variable — it decides whether we land inside or outside the
  historical range." *(e.g. the Industrial-Revolution comparison and where it likely breaks.)*
- 🔴 **Normative / design (values + robustness).** What we must choose, not predict. Held to a
  robustness standard: which arrangements hold up across a wide range of possible futures, not
  "this future will happen so prepare for it."

**The cardinal rule: never dress a 🔴 or 🟡 as a 🟢.** Credibility — not cleverness — is the
only capital an independent, non-credentialed scholar can compound. Calibrated honesty is the
scarce resource here. (This rule was learned the hard way; see `../LESSONS.md`.)

## What "success" looks like

Not one big paper. A **sustained body of work** that compounds:
- Frequent public writing (Medium), each piece honest about which claims are which.
- Mostly 🟡/🔴 essays (frameworks, analogies, policy/culture reasoning) — the volume.
- Periodically, a 🟢 empirical "load-bearing pillar" (a small falsifiable study or honest
  re-analysis of real data) — roughly one or two a year — so the essays stand on data, not air.
- Over time, a few signature concepts others can cite — the move from "person who writes
  essays" to "person who proposed a framework."

The long-term aim: to contribute, honestly, to how humanity thinks about meaning after work.

## How this repository works as the hub

This repo is the project's long-term memory (the AI assistant's memory resets each session;
the repo does not). Everything worth keeping lives here:
- `MANIFESTO.md` — this file. The north star.
- `evidence_base.md` — the running catalogue of 🟢 empirical anchors.
- `idea_pool.md` — incubating article ideas.
- `concepts.md` — the project's own coinages / frameworks as they develop.
- `STYLE.md` — the author's voice + Medium conventions, so drafts come out aligned.
- `articles/` — one folder per Medium piece (draft, evidence, figures).

## Lineage

This project grew out of, and supersedes, two earlier efforts kept in this repo for history:
the unfalsifiable post-scarcity ABM (archived in `../archive/abm_v1/`), and the falsifiable
Stack Overflow null-result study (`../so_sdt_study/`). The ABM taught the lesson *polishing ≠ fixing an
unfalsifiable foundation*; the SO study proved the author can do honest, pre-registered,
placebo-controlled empirical work — and can serve as a 🟢 pillar / case study in the writing.
